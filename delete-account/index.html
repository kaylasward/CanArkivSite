Account controls

Delete your CanArkiv account.

You can delete your account inside CanArkiv. If you can't access the app, you can also request deletion by email.

Delete inside the app

  1. Open CanArkiv and sign in.
  2. Go to your profile and open Settings.
  3. Find the account deletion option and follow the on-screen confirmation steps.

Deleting an account is permanent. Review the confirmation shown in the app before proceeding.

Request deletion without the app

Email support@canarkiv.com with the subject “Account deletion request” and identify the email address linked to your CanArkiv account. We may ask you to confirm ownership before acting on the request.

Request account deletion ↗

If you have a question about which account data is affected, contact support before confirming deletion.

Account security

Please don't send credentials.

Do not include a password, verification code, recovery link, or other sensitive sign-in information in your email.

Requests made by email require account ownership verification; they aren't an instant, unauthenticated deletion endpoint.
