ACCOUNT & DATA

Delete your account.

You can permanently delete your CanArkiv account in the app, or request deletion without accessing the app.

From the app

  1. Open CanArkiv and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Choose the account deletion option and follow the confirmation steps.

Account deletion is permanent. Review the in-app confirmation before continuing.

Without the app

If you can't access CanArkiv, you can still request account deletion. Send a message to the address below with the subject CanArkiv account deletion request. Include the email linked to your account. We may ask you to verify ownership.

support@canarkiv.com

This address accepts deletion requests even if you have uninstalled the app. Don't send a password, sign-in code, or recovery link.
