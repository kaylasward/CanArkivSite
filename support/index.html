Common questions

Can I browse without an account?

Yes. You can explore the drink catalog without signing in. An account is needed to save drinks, post reviews, and contribute to the archive.

What if a barcode doesn't find anything?

Try searching by brand or flavor. If the drink is missing, you can submit it through the app after signing in.

Why isn't my submitted photo visible?

Community photos are reviewed before they appear publicly. You can check your own submissions in the app.
