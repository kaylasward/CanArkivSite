A few common questions

Can I browse without an account?

Yes. You can explore the drink catalog without signing in. To track drinks or contribute, you'll need an account.

What if a barcode doesn't find anything?

Not every barcode is in the archive yet. You can search for the drink by name and contribute missing information.

Why isn't my submitted photo visible to everyone?

New community photos are reviewed before being shown publicly. You can still see your own pending submissions.

How do I delete my account?

Use the account deletion option in the app, or see the account deletion page for a request method outside the app.

Please note

Keep private details private.

Never email us your password, sign-in code, access token, or payment information. We won't need those to understand an app issue.
