FAQ

Can I browse without signing in?

Yes. You can explore energy drinks without an account. Sign in to save drinks to your lists, write reviews, or contribute to the archive.

What if I can't find a drink I want to save?

Try searching by brand or flavor, or scan the barcode. If the drink isn't listed, you can submit it using the Add tab in the app. You'll need to sign in, and new submissions are reviewed.

How do I add a photo or correct drink details?

Open the drink in the app and tap the three-dot menu. From there, choose Add Photo, Add or Edit Details, or Suggest a Correction.

Why isn't my submission visible yet?

Community contributions are reviewed before they're shown publicly. You can check their status under Profile → Submissions.

Account Settings

In the app, open Profile → Settings to edit your profile or access account settings. To delete your account, select Account & Security → Delete Account.

Can't access the app? You can also request account deletion without signing in.
