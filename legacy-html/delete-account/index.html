Delete Your Account.

You can delete your CanArkiv account in the app. If you no longer have access, you can request deletion without the app.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Sign in to CanArkiv.
  2. Open Profile and tap the Settings icon.
  3. Go to Account & Security → Delete Account and follow the confirmation steps.

Deletion can't be undone. The app shows what will be deleted before you confirm.

What gets deleted?

Your profile, ratings, reviews, drink lists, reports, and uploaded photos are deleted. Catalog records and barcode information you've contributed may remain in the archive, without a link to your account.

Request deletion without the app

If you've uninstalled CanArkiv or can't access your account, contact support@canarkiv.com with the subject CanArkiv account deletion request. Include the email address linked to your account. We may ask you to verify ownership.

support@canarkiv.com

Don't send your password, verification code, or recovery link. You don't need to reinstall the app to make a deletion request.
